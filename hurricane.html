Restoring healthcare services and supporting communities affected by a catastrophic Category 5 hurricane.

Recovery Overview

Our Recovery Mission

The purpose of this recovery initiative is to support the reconstruction, restocking, and restoration of essential medical services at Black River Hospital in St. Elizabeth, Jamaica, following a devastating hurricane.

Hospital Reconstruction

Repair damaged buildings, restore electrical systems, improve water supply, and prepare medical spaces for safe operation.

Medical Restocking

Replenish medicines, medical equipment, surgical supplies, protective equipment, and other essential hospital materials.

Community Healthcare

Support healthcare workers and help restore reliable medical services for families throughout St. Elizabeth.

Hospital Reconstruction Priorities

Rebuilding requires careful assessment and the restoration of critical hospital infrastructure.

1. Structural Repairs
  • Inspect damaged buildings.
  • Repair roofs and ceilings.
  • Replace broken windows and doors.
  • Check walls and foundations.
2. Electricity and Power
  • Repair electrical systems.
  • Inspect generators.
  • Restore emergency lighting.
  • Protect critical medical equipment.
3. Water and Sanitation
  • Restore clean water supply.
  • Repair plumbing systems.
  • Restore sanitation facilities.
  • Improve drainage systems.
4. Medical Facilities
  • Restore emergency rooms.
  • Repair operating theatres.
  • Prepare patient wards.
  • Restore laboratory services.
5. Ventilation and Safety
  • Inspect air conditioning.
  • Check medical gas systems.
  • Improve infection prevention.
  • Inspect fire safety equipment.
6. Security and Access
  • Clear debris from entrances.
  • Restore safe access roads.
  • Repair security systems.
  • Protect hospital supplies.
